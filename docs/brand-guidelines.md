# Still Dental art direction

Concept: room to feel at ease. Fictional Karachi practice, visibly a demo. Palette: warm cream #f7f6ee, sage #e0e8db, forest #24483e, terracotta #bf7459.
Typography: local Plus Jakarta Sans headings and DM Sans body. Display 42–72px, body 16–19px.
Grid: 1180px max width, 32px desktop gutters, 20px mobile. Spacing 8/16/24/36/56/80/104px.
Hero: generous cream canvas, approachable dental photograph in a curved frame with a compact visit note; small organic motif drawn in SVG.
Navigation: welcoming wordmark with an original petal mark, simple care links, soft rectangular booking button. Mobile disclosure and prominent booking action.
Imagery: licensed dental stock images explicitly illustrative, demo staff portraits clearly labeled.
Motion: calm opacity reveals; preserve fully visible content with reduced motion.
Distinctive elements: soft botanical mark, curved photography with visit note, two-column booking wizard with persistent appointment summary.
Tokens: forest/sage/cream → text/background/muted → booking panel, selected slot and button variables.
Forms: visible labels and native controls, progress steps, error/status feedback, clearly labeled timezone and local demo confirmation.
